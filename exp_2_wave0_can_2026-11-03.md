- Reading: on can, w=0 at k>=1 (0.53-0.82) reproduces the untrained step-0 det evals (0.55-0.78). Most of the early fixed-k success is the sampler change, not RL, so compare each fixed-k run against its own zero row. The gauss sanity check FAILS: gauss is not equal to base. Can k=1 gives 0.665, about 8 SE above 0.375; square k=1 gives 0.645 against 0.505; transport k=3-8 gives 0.24-0.27 against 0.195. The reason is in adsrl/sampler.py: at step k it sets x = sqrt(abar_prev)*x0_hat + sqrt(1-abar_prev)*w. That re-noises the posterior mean x0_hat, not a sample of x0, so it is sharper than the true marginal (mode-seeking) even for Gaussian w. So it is not DDPM resampling. At k=0, gauss is the same distribution as base on a different noise stream. Can gives 0.485 there against 0.375, a gap of ~2.3 SE, which is probably eval noise; base would need a second seed to check. w=0 at k=0 (constant x_T=0) is near zero on all tasks; square w=0 is below base for k=1-3 (0.36-0.48), unlike can. On transport, w=0 is above gauss at k>=5.

- **The true base success is 0.42 ± 0.013.** The earlier 0.375 and 0.485 (n=200, SE about 0.034) are within 1.4 and 1.9 SE of it, so the gap between them is sampling noise. The eval settings are the same across base_eval, base_eval_wk, AdsrlCallback.evaluate, cem_noise and upstream: 300 env steps (75 chunks of 4), success = any chunk with reward > -1, obs normalized by the same robomimic_lowdim wrapper, and x_T redrawn every chunk.
- **Upstream's 7/10 does not measure the base policy.** dsrl_na feeds the actor's tanh-squashed sample as the noise. That noise is bounded to [-1, 1] (std about 0.63), and its mean comes from the randomly initialized actor, so it differs by seed. Smaller noise alone raises success to 0.70. The seed-dependent mean then moves the result between 0.33 and 0.73 across seeds, even with 500 episodes each.
- The 20-episode 0.70 spot check used the same protocol. Getting 14/20 when the true rate is 0.42 has p ≈ 0.01. I found no settings difference that explains it, so it is most likely an unlucky small sample.
- **The w=0, k=1 gain is real (+0.40, more than 30 SE) and needs no RL.** It is consistent with the tanh row: this base policy scores higher with less noise. RL gains at k≥1 should be compared against 0.83, not 0.42.
- **Eval-protocol bug (not fixed):** `eval_env.seed()` has no effect on initial states. SB3 passes `reset(seed=...)`, but `ObservationWrapperRobomimic.reset` only reads `options["seed"]` (upstream `dsrl/env_utils.py`, same in dppo's wrapper). As a result, initial states come from each worker's unseeded global np RNG. Episodes are i.i.d. across envs, so the estimates are unbiased, but evals cannot be reproduced and "same episode seeds" comparisons (base_eval_wk comment, CEM, the spot check) are not paired. Reproduced by `tests/test_eval_seeding.py::test_eval_env_seed_controls_initial_states` (fails; the distinct-across-envs test passes).
